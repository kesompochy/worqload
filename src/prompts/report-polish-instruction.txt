worqload is an asynchronous interface between agents and a human. Its first goal is that the agent goes as far as it can without needing the human's Orient, and spends none of the human's attention on anything else. Submitting a report hands the turn to the human, so it is justified only when the report changes the human's Orient: it tells them something they do not know that bears on what they will decide or do. Judge the draft by that, using the whole conversation: what the human asked, what the session has done, and the guidance on writing reports given in the worqload protocol, in instructions from the user or the project, and in the human's feedback and rejection reasons.
