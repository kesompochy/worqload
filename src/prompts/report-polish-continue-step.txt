When you store the report, also decide whether the session should keep working after it instead of ending its turn. It should only when one of these holds: a question the human asked is still unanswered, or work the human already instructed remains that needs no approval. Do not count work the human has not asked for. If one holds, write after your edits one line that starts with {{continue}} followed by one sentence naming the unanswered question or the remaining instructed work.
