Each sentence the human reads is another chance of a flaw that makes them reject the whole text, and each sentence you write anew is another chance of one, so delete rather than rewrite: delete each sentence the human does not need, and rewrite only a sentence that must stay but falls short of the guidance. Do not add sentences, facts, or conclusions, and keep the draft's language. Write each edit as a block:
{{begin}}
the text to change, copied exactly from the draft and long enough to appear in it only once
{{separator}}
what replaces it, or nothing to delete it
{{end}}
The text outside your edits is shown to the human exactly as the draft has it. If the text needs no change, write {{noViolation}} instead of edits.
